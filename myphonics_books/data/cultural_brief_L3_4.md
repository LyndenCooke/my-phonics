# CULTURAL BRIEF: L3.4 (oi, aw — title TBD)

Region: Seoul, South Korea (Jongno-gu — ordinary neighbourhood within sight of Bukchon's hanok rooftops)
Setting: Modern primary school art room + walk home down a ginkgo-lined street + family apartment. Autumn.

## CONTEMPORARY REALITY CHECK
- A normal day for a 6-7 year old in Seoul RIGHT NOW: wakes in a modern high-rise apartment, walks or is walked to a modern state primary school (chodeunghakgyo) in casual clothes with a colourful backpack; classes include art; after school many children go to a care classroom or an after-school class (art, coding, taekwondo) before home; snack at home; play, homework, screens.
- A visitor on an ordinary street sees: clean pavements, mid- and high-rise apartment blocks, small shops with Hangul signage (we render signs BLANK — no lettering rule), parked cars and delivery scooters, golden ginkgo trees in autumn, school children in bright casual clothes; in Jongno, traditional tiled hanok rooftops appear BETWEEN modern buildings — the old and new genuinely share the skyline.
- Contemporary-first? YES. School and apartment are modern; hanok rooftops and one courtyard glimpse are the cultural seasoning, exactly as they are in real Jongno daily life.

## CLOTHING
- Child (everyday): casual modern clothes — NO uniform at state primary schools (uniforms start at middle school). Hero girl: e.g. mustard-yellow long-sleeve top under a coral padded gilet, navy leggings under a corduroy skirt (modesty: legs always covered), white trainers, small art smock in class.
- Boy classmate: e.g. green sweatshirt, dark joggers, trainers.
- Child (traditional occasion): NOT used — no festival in this story; hanbok would be wrong for a school day.
- Adult (teacher): modern smart-casual (cardigan, trousers).
- Modesty note: leggings/trousers under any skirt; long sleeves fit the autumn season naturally.

## ARCHITECTURE / INTERIOR
- Modern reality: school art room with low tables, small chairs, art trolley, paper on easels/table, big windows showing autumn trees and apartment blocks. Apartment home: warm modern living room/kitchen, floor cushions at a low table are authentic alongside a sofa; shoes off at the door (entry shelf with shoes = lovely accurate detail).
- Cultural texture: on the walk home, grey-tiled hanok rooftops with gently curved eaves visible between modern buildings; one low stone/wood hanok wall with a wooden gate along the street; golden ginkgo leaves on the pavement. NO postcard-only hanok village scenes.
- Objects: metal chopsticks + spoon at meals, small floor table (if home meal shown), child's drawings held to the fridge with magnets.

## LANDSCAPE / ENVIRONMENT
- Autumn in Seoul: brilliant blue sky, golden ginkgo and red maple street trees, leaves on pavements; Namsan Seoul Tower may appear tiny on a distant hill (nice, real, optional); mountains faint on the horizon (Seoul is ringed by them).
- Weather: crisp, bright; light jackets/gilets.
- Animals: small dogs on leads are common; magpies (Korea's beloved bird) in street trees.

## FOOD (if relevant)
- After-school snack at home: hotteok (golden sweet pancake, brown-sugar-cinnamon filling) — classic autumn/winter snack a parent makes; or gyeran-ppang (egg bread). Fruit: Korean pear slices, persimmon (autumn!).
- If a meal appears: rice, soup, small side dishes (banchan) in little bowls, metal chopsticks + long spoon.

## CULTURAL NOTES
- Family terms: Mum = Eomma, Dad = Appa, Grandma = Halmeoni, Grandpa = Harabeoji. (Decodability warning: these words are NOT decodable at L3 — use only if listed as story words, else use "Mum".)
- School: art class is a real weekly subject; after-school art classes are common and beloved.
- Children's drawing culture: kids draw at low tables, on floors; praise culture around effort.
- Sounds of the street: magpies, delivery scooters, school chatter.

## STEREOTYPE WARNINGS
- AVOID: hanbok on a school day; postcard-only hanok village as the entire world; palace guards; kimchi-as-identity gags; K-pop shorthand; chopstick-fumbling jokes; "exotic Orient" colour grading (keep light natural and autumn-golden).
- AVOID poverty or hyper-neon Blade-Runner Seoul — the ordinary truth is clean, leafy, modern residential streets.
- The hanok texture must coexist with modern buildings in the SAME frame at least once — never imply Koreans live in a heritage museum.

## CONTEMPORARY BALANCE VERDICT
- Would a Seoul child recognise their daily life? YES — casual clothes, modern school art room, apartment home, after-school snack, ginkgo autumn walk.
- Showing the culture as it IS, not as the West imagines it? YES.
- Tradition as seasoning, not the meal? YES — hanok rooftops/gate glimpsed on the walk home only.

## DIGNITY CHECK
- Would someone from Seoul feel proud? YES — warm, orderly, beautiful, contemporary; the emotional story (criticism → sorry → drawing together) is universal and handled gently.
- A real place where real people live well? YES.

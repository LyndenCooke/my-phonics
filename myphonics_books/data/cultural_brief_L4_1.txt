CULTURAL BRIEF: The Purple Purse (L4.1)
Region: Istanbul, Turkey (Kadıköy/Moda neighbourhood — Asian side)
Setting: Modern residential neighbourhood with parks, shops, and historic stone walls

CONTEMPORARY REALITY CHECK:
- What does a normal day look like for a child here RIGHT NOW? Walk to school past modern apartment buildings, play in parks, visit corner shops (bakkal), wear casual modern clothes. Istanbul is a major modern metropolis — children's daily life is very similar to London or Paris.
- What would a visitor see walking down an ordinary street? Mid-rise apartment blocks (5-7 floors, cream/warm tones), corner shops with colourful awnings, cafés, parked cars, green parks, cats everywhere, modern trams, some historic stone walls and churches mixed into the modern streetscape.
- Is this setting contemporary-first? YES — modern neighbourhood with historic texture, not tourist Istanbul.

CLOTHING:
- Child (everyday): Modern casual — jumpers, jeans, trainers, light jackets. For this book: purple jumper with gold star pattern, dark navy jeans, white trainers, light blue zip-up jacket.
- Dad: Modern casual — dark casual jacket, grey shirt, dark jeans, trainers. Dark brown hair (short), light olive skin, clean-shaven or light stubble.
- Herb seller: Middle-aged woman, headscarf (modern style), warm cardigan over long dress with trousers underneath. Friendly face.
- Shopkeeper: Middle-aged man, casual shirt, apron.
- Modesty note: All characters fully covered. No bare legs, no shorts. Dresses with trousers/leggings underneath.

ARCHITECTURE/INTERIOR:
- Modern reality: Mid-rise apartment buildings painted in cream, white, or warm tones. Modern windows, balconies with plants. Clean, well-maintained streets with parked cars and lamp posts.
- Cultural texture: Some Ottoman-era stone walls, small neighbourhood mosque visible in skyline, distinctive Turkish corner shops (bakkal) with colourful displays and awnings. Cats sitting on walls.
- Interiors: Modern apartment hallway — coat hooks, shoe rack, tiled floor.

LANDSCAPE/ENVIRONMENT:
- Green parks with mature trees, benches, ferns growing in shaded areas
- Cats are a distinctive Istanbul feature — one might appear in backgrounds
- Mediterranean climate — mild, green, partly cloudy
- Colourful buildings (not all white — Istanbul neighbourhoods use warm tones, pastels)
- Street trees, flower boxes on balconies

FOOD (relevant):
- Herb stall selling fresh herbs: mint, parsley, dill, thyme (all common Turkish herbs)
- The herb stall is a modern street-side stall, NOT a traditional Grand Bazaar stall
- Fresh herbs sold in bunches, displayed in wooden crates

CULTURAL NOTES:
- Family warmth: Turkish families are close-knit, fathers are actively involved with children
- Neighbourhood feel: Istanbul's Asian-side neighbourhoods (Kadıköy, Moda) are walkable, friendly, and distinctly modern
- Corner shops (bakkal) are a genuine daily-life feature
- Cats are beloved and everywhere — a culturally distinctive detail

STEREOTYPE WARNINGS:
- AVOID: Grand Bazaar, flying carpet imagery, Ottoman palace tourism
- AVOID: Fez, turban, or other costume stereotypes
- AVOID: Desert imagery (Istanbul is GREEN and coastal)
- AVOID: Only showing heritage architecture (mosques, palaces) — show modern buildings FIRST
- AVOID: "Exotic marketplace" framing — the herb stall is an ordinary neighbourhood feature
- Historic elements (stone walls, small mosque in skyline) appear as texture, not as the main setting

CONTEMPORARY BALANCE VERDICT:
- Would a child FROM this culture recognise their daily life in this book? YES — modern neighbourhood, casual clothes, corner shop, park
- Are we showing the culture as it IS, not as the West imagines it? YES — no bazaar tourism, no costume stereotypes
- Does tradition appear as seasoning within modern life, not as the entire meal? YES — stone wall, small church/mosque in skyline, herb stall as texture

DIGNITY CHECK:
- Would someone from Istanbul feel proud seeing this? YES — shows their city as modern, vibrant, walkable, green, and full of character
- Does this feel like a REAL place where REAL people live well? YES — comfortable, middle-class, warm neighbourhood

IMAGE FEASIBILITY CHECK:
1. Can each page have a DIFFERENT background/location? YES — apartment, street, park, shop, stone wall, herb stall, walking home
2. Does any page require two things that must visually match? NO — only the purple purse recurs as a simple object
3. Is there any recurring background detail that must stay identical? NO — different location each page
4. Can the main visual element on each page stand alone? YES

Guide for Grown-Ups
This is your child's first decodable book! Every word uses only the sounds they have learnt. Read it together and celebrate their phonics journey.
Before Reading
Look at the cover and read the title together.
Practise the sounds on page 3 before starting.
During Reading
Point to each word as your child reads.
If stuck, help them sound out each letter.
Praise the effort, not just accuracy!
After Reading
Talk about what happened in the story.
Read it again another day for confidence.
"I love how you kept trying!" matters more than "Well done." Celebrate persistence and enjoy reading together.

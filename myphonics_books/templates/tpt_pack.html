How to Use This Pack
A 4-step teaching sequence

A short teaching sequence.

The worksheets and book are designed to be used in this order. Each step builds on the last, taking children from sound recognition through to comprehension.

1
Warm up the sounds (Worksheets 1–3)
Recap SATPIN, push the sound buttons under each phoneme, and blend simple CVC words. Builds the phonemic awareness children need before reading the book.
2
Read "Tap! Tap! Tap!" together (Storybook)
Read the included book aloud, then have children read it independently. Every word is decodable from SATPIN plus the tricky words "I" and "the".
3
Decode and read (Worksheets 4–6)
Tackle alien (nonsense) words to check pure decoding, read short decodable sentences, and reinforce the tricky words "I" and "the" in context.
4
Write the sounds and words (Worksheets 7–9)
Form the tricky words, practise letter formation for s, a, t, p, then apply those letters in real CVC words from the story.
5
Check comprehension (Worksheet 10)
Children sequence the story in their own drawings — a quick, low-stakes comprehension check that doubles as creative practice.

Printing the Storybook
Two formats included

Printing & folding.

Two book PDFs are included so you can use whichever fits your printer setup. Both contain the same story.

Option A · Read-aloud (A5)

  1. Open Tap Tap Tap — Read Aloud.pdf.
  2. Print on A5 paper, or print on A4 and select Fit to page.
  3. Pages come out in story order — great for reading on the carpet or 1:1.
  4. Staple along the left edge if you want a simple booklet.

Option B · Print & Fold Booklet (A4)

  1. Open Tap Tap Tap — Print & Fold Booklet.pdf.
  2. Print on A4 paper. Select Print on both sides — Flip on short edge.
  3. Fold each sheet in half so the pages line up.
  4. Staple twice along the fold to make a saddle-stitched A5 booklet.
Printer tip: If you don't have access to A5 paper, both PDFs print fine on A4 — your printer will scale them. Look for the Fit / Scale to page option in your print dialog. Greyscale printing is supported, but the cover looks best in colour.
Classroom copies: You are licensed to print as many copies as you need for the children in your own classroom. Please don't share the files with other teachers — send them this listing instead. (See page 16 for full terms.)

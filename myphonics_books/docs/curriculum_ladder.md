# Curriculum Ladder (8-Level Scheme, v2.1 — realigned 2026-06-08)

Each level introduces new sounds. Books at each level are numbered to ensure systematic coverage.

**Realignment note:** the original 6-level scheme was restructured on 2026-06-08. Old Level 1 (10 books) split into new L1/L2/L3; old L2-L6 shifted to new L4-L8. Stories and image assets are still keyed by their ORIGINAL ids — see `NEW_TO_OLD` in `scripts/generate_pilot_books.py`. Level names, colours and year groups: `scripts/generate_book.py` and `data/graphemes_by_level.json`.

**Positioning:** L6 is the target for meeting Year 2 expectations; L7-L8 are greater depth for high achievers.

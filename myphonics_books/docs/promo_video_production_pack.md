# MyPhonicsBooks Promo Video — Production Pack

**Format:** 3:4 portrait (Instagram and Facebook feed; switch to 9:16 for Reels)
**Length:** 30 seconds, six clips
**Angle:** Parent problem-solution
**Generation:** `py -3.12 scripts/generate_promo_clips.py` (fal.ai, Seedance 2.0, ~$7 fast tier)

---

## Script and Voiceover

| Time | Beat | Voiceover (British English, warm mum-to-mum tone) |
|------|------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 0-4s | Hook | "If your child guesses at words instead of reading them, it is not your child. It is the book." |
| 4-9s | Problem | "Generic reading books ignore the sounds your child has actually learned. So they guess, they struggle and they start to dread reading time." |
| 9-16s | Mechanism | "MyPhonicsBooks builds every book around the exact sounds your child knows. Every single word is decodable at their level. No guessing. Just reading." |
| 16-23s | Proof | "Eight levels, from first sounds to Reading Champion. Each story a window to a different culture. Print-ready tonight." |
| 23-30s | CTA | "Take the free three minute assessment and get five personalised books, free. Link below." |

## Notes

- House style is non-negotiable in illustrated shots: flat colours, clean lines, small solid black dot eyes. Never big or wide eyes. Review clip 3 and 4 against this before publishing.
- Voiceover: record yourself for the teacher-built positioning; AI voice as fallback.
- Edit in CapCut or Canva; clips butt-join in script order.
- Cost control: fast tier first; rerun any weak clip with `--clip N --std`.

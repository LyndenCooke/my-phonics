# Lesson 1 · Meet the sound: s — one-take filming outline

Target 10-12 minutes. No editing. Glance at this, teach, upload.
Props on desk before you press record: sound card **s** · Sound Book **s**
(sun photos) · whiteboard + pen. Frame tight enough to see the card.

## Run sheet

**0:00 · Cold open (20 sec)**
Straight in, card face down: "This is Lesson 1 of the whole learn-to-read
journey. Ten minutes a day, one small step at a time. Today your child meets
their very first sound."

**0:30 · Sound talk warm-up (2 min)**
Robot voice game, no letters anywhere yet.
- "My turn: s-u-n." … "Your turn." → sun
- Repeat: s-a-t → sat · s-i-p → sip
- To camera: "Pause here and play three rounds with your child."

**2:30 · Hear it (2 min)**
- "My turn: /sss/. Your turn." Twice.
- Mouth close to camera: teeth together, air hisses. Stretch it: sssss.
- Say the rule out loud: "/sss/, never suh. No extra sound on the end."

**4:30 · See it (3 min)**
- Open Sound Book s: sun photos, say /sss/ each time the letter appears.
- Sound card up: say it together, then cold. "Card up… wait… /sss/!"
- "The letter is the picture of the sound. If they say 'ess', lovely, that is
  its name; today we say the sound: /sss/."
- Pause-point: "Pause and do five cold flashes."

**7:30 · Form it (3 min)**
- Patter twice, slowly, drawing huge in the air: "Start at the top, curve
  round the snake's head, then swing back the other way."
- Sky-write together three times.
- Whiteboard: "Pause and write three. Watch the first stroke: start at the
  top; the curve loves to go the wrong way."

**10:30 · Prove it (1 min)**
- "Show me /sss/": say m … sss … sun … dog … sock; they signal on /s/.
- Teaser: "s-a-t… tomorrow we blend that into a word."

**11:30 · Outro (30 sec)**
- Home task: hunt five /sss/ things, sky-write s three times.
- "Lesson 2: we blend s into first words. Subscribe so you do not lose your
  place on the journey."
- Funnel line: "The s practice sheet is free below; it comes from the Level 1
  workbook if you want the whole thing done for you."

## Do not
Say "suh" · lead with the letter name · show written words today (voices
only) · run past 12 minutes.

# Reel · Lesson 1 launch — one-take outline (30-40 sec, vertical)

You decide the shots; this is the spine. Phone at eye level, sound card s in
hand, whiteboard within reach. One take, no editing.

## Hook (pick one, first 2 seconds)
1. "Your child's entire reading life starts with one sound."
2. "I'm a primary teacher and I'm going to teach your child to read. Free. From lesson one."
3. Card held to lens: "This is the first thing every reader learns."

## Beats
- **0:02** "This is /sss/. Not suh. /sss/." (mouth close, teeth together, hiss)
- **0:08** "Play this first: my turn, s-u-n. Your turn… sun. That is blending,
  and it starts before letters do."
- **0:16** Card flash: "See it, say it: /sss/."
- **0:20** Air-write huge: "Start at the top, curve round the snake's head,
  swing back the other way."
- **0:28** "That is Lesson 1 of the whole journey: Reception to fluent
  reader, ten minutes a day, taught in order."
- **0:34** CTA: "Full lesson free on my YouTube. Lesson 2 is blending. Follow
  so your child does not miss a step."

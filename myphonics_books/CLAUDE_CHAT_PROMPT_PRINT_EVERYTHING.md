I run MyPhonicsBooks, a complete UK systematic synthetic phonics programme
(Reception to Year 2, eight colour-banded levels, Letters and Sounds based,
my own terminology throughout). Everything exists as print-ready PDFs. I
want to print every resource at least once so I can hold the whole
programme physically, sort it, spot weaknesses and decide what ships to
schools and parents. I need you to be my thinking partner for planning that
print run and the physical review. Here is the full inventory:

**Books**
- 33 decodable storybooks across 8 levels (L1 Ditties 2, L2 First Sounds 5,
  L3 Special Friends 3, L4 Longer Sounds 6, L5 New Spellings 5, L6 Building
  Fluency 4, L7 Reading Together 4, L8 Reading Champion 4). A5 booklets,
  16-24pp, full colour.
- 70 Sound Books (one real-photo book per sound, A4, roughly 10-16pp each):
  L1 ×10, L2 ×15, L3 ×6, L4 ×12, L5 ×10, L6 ×9, L7 ×6, L8 ×2. Covers are
  due a redesign, so these may be printed last or draft-quality.
- 12 Sound Blending Books (L1-L5, A4 colour booklets).

**Workbooks and worksheets**
- 8 companion workbooks, one per level (A4, 25-30pp, colour).
- 33 six-page worksheet packs, one per storybook (handwriting, spelling,
  grammar, sentence writing, book-linked activity, review).

**Card decks (all A4 sheets to cut, ideally 250-300gsm, laminated)**
- Main sound card deck: 74 main + 19 shifty + 32 extra sounds + 7-card ough
  pack (premium tier and free-printable tier).
- 169 picture-word cards (L1-L2 focus).
- Word decks L1-L8 (blending words per level).
- Tricky word cards per level.

**Assessment**
- Quick Level Check placement packs, one per level plus a combined all-levels
  version.
- 16 half-termly progress checks plus class trackers.
- Phonics Screening Check mocks A and B (pupil and teacher versions each).
- Keep-Up Kit (1:1 intervention routine guide).

**Teacher-facing**
- 8 Teacher's Guides, one per level (A4 booklets).
- Lesson overview pages (one A4 page per lesson; 462 planned, first ones
  exist).
- 8 marketing flagship booklets (one per level, 16-20pp, A5).

What I want from this conversation:

1. A sensible print strategy: what to print at home versus a print shop,
   what can be mono or draft quality for review purposes versus what must be
   full colour and proper stock, and a rough cost-sanity view for a one-copy
   review run of this size.
2. A physical organisation system: how to box, folder or wallet everything
   so it is sorted by level and by resource type at the same time, so a
   teacher could walk up to it and find things. I think in systems, so
   suggest something I can later replicate as the actual school pack.
3. A review workflow: a practical checklist for going through hundreds of
   printed pieces without losing track. What to check per resource type
   (colour accuracy, cut lines, decodability slips, cover quality,
   consistency of the level colour bands), how to log faults and how to
   batch fixes afterwards.
4. Ask me questions where my inventory is ambiguous rather than assuming.

Context that matters: the eight levels each have a fixed colour (L1 pink
#E84B8A, L2 coral #F97066, L3 amber #F59E0B, L4 green #22C55E, L5 blue
#3B82F6, L6 indigo #6366F1, L7 purple #8B5CF6, L8 teal #14B8A6) and colour
consistency across a level is one of the things I am checking physically.
British English throughout. Practical suggestions I can act on this week,
not theory.

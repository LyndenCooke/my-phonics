## 1. What we are printing

The marketing is already built into each booklet (a "Find Your Level" check and a two-page library back cover with QR codes); no extra pages are being added.

We want quotes for two options:

- **Option A:** 8 flagship booklets, one per reading level (Level 1 to Level 8)

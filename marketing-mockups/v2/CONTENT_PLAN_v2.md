# MyPhonicsBooks: Marketing Content Plan v2

## Positioning: What We Actually Sell

We do not sell phonics books. We do not sell a reading scheme. We do not sell levels.

We sell these outcomes:

1. **"My child can read English confidently."**
2. **"I know exactly where my child is with reading."**
3. **"My child is learning the same way British children do."**
4. **"I started them off right and I can see the progress."**

The books are the mechanism. The assessment is the entry point. The outcome is the sale.


## Target Audiences (in priority order)

**Audience A: Expat families (primary)**
British families living abroad, or international families who want British-standard English for their children. They fear their child is falling behind. They don't have access to a British school. They want the same system. They want reassurance. They want a plan.

Pain: "Are they keeping up?" "I don't know what level they should be at." "There's no British school here."
Desire: "I want them to read English like a British child." "I want to give them the same start."

**Audience B: UK parents of children aged 4-6**
Children in Reception or Year 1. Parents who want to support phonics at home but don't know how. They hear about phonics but don't understand the system.

Pain: "I don't know how to help them." "I don't know if they're where they should be." "School isn't enough."
Desire: "I want to do something at home." "I want to know their reading age." "I want to start them off right."

**Audience C: Home educators and EAL families**
Parents who are the primary literacy teacher. Families where English is a second or third language. They need structure, not just books.

Pain: "I don't know where to start." "I don't have a curriculum." "English isn't my first language."
Desire: "A system I can follow." "My child will be secure in English." "I can do this myself."

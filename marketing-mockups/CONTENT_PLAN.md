# MyPhonicsBooks Content Marketing Plan

## Campaign: "Every Word They Can Read"

**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, Meta Ads
**Funnel:** Awareness > Consideration > Conversion
**Duration:** 8-week rolling cycle (repeat with fresh creative)
**Primary audience:** UK parents of children aged 4 to 8
**Secondary audience:** Home educators, EAL families, international British curriculum families


## Brand Rules (Apply to Everything)

**Voice:** Warm, encouraging, knowledgeable. Like a friendly Year 1 teacher at pick-up time.

**Language:** British English. No Oxford commas. No em dashes. No emojis.

**Visual rules:** No realistic human faces. Use book covers, book illustrations, flat lays, typography-first designs. Level colours throughout.

**Meta compliance:** Never "your child" (use "they" or the child's name). No urgency or pressure. No faces in ad imagery.

**Copy formula:** Pain > Solution > Proof > CTA

**Core fear:** "My child is behind."
**Core passion:** "My child will read confidently."

**Trust phrases:** "Aligned with the UK phonics curriculum." "Based on Letters and Sounds." "Every word matched to their reading level." "Designed by phonics specialists."

**Legal footer (where space allows):** "Based on Letters and Sounds (DfE 2007). Not affiliated with any commercial phonics programme."

## Video/Reel Scripts

### Reel 1: "The Guessing Game" (15 seconds)
**Hook (0-3s):** Text on screen: "Most reading books are a guessing game."
**Problem (3-7s):** Show a word like "knight". Text: "If they haven't learned 'kn', how can they read this?"
**Solution (7-12s):** Show a MyPhonicsBooks cover. Text: "Every word uses only the sounds they know."
**CTA (12-15s):** "Free assessment. Link in bio."

No music. Voiceover or text only. No faces.

### Reel 2: "6 Levels in 15 Seconds" (15 seconds)
**Each level appears for 2 seconds:** Cover + level colour + level name.
Quick cuts through all 6 levels.
**Final 3 seconds:** "Which level are they on? Find out in 3 minutes."

No music. Sound effects optional (page turn, gentle tap). No faces.

### Reel 3: "Open Window" (30 seconds)
**Hook (0-3s):** "Every book is an open window."
**Sequence (3-24s):** Show 6 covers one by one. Japan. Kenya. Ghana. Istanbul. Mexico. Egypt. Each with country name overlay.
**Close (24-30s):** "30 stories. 6 levels. Every word decodable. MyPhonicsBooks."

No music. No faces. Ambient sounds from each culture optional but not required.

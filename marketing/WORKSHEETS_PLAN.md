# Worksheet plan, Levels 1 to 8

Written 2026-09-20. Generated by `scripts/worksheets/plan.py`, built by
`scripts/worksheets/forge_batch.py` with the offline worksheet forge
(`worksheet-forge/`, no AI, decodability guard on every word).

## The rule

A worksheet is one purpose, delivered as a short sequence of tasks in a fixed
order. Reference pages (sound mats, story-word lists) are not worksheets.
Every pack that belongs to a story links the story, on the site and in the
Facebook caption.

## Progression across the levels

| Level | What the child is doing | Sheet types per book |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Forming and hearing single sounds and digraphs | Trace and write (one per sound), Hear and build, Read draw write, Story game |
| 4 | Hearing and reading vowel digraphs in context | Hear and build + Read in sentences (per sound), Check-up, Story game |
| 5 | Choosing between spellings of a sound | Spell it (best bet) + Read in sentences (per sound), Check-up, Story game |
| 6 | Automatic reading of alternative spellings | Spell it + Crack the code (per sound), Sentences, Story game |
| 7 | Fluency with trigraphs and -tion | Spell it + Fluency (per sound), Sentences, Story game |
| 8 | Suffix morphology | Spell it + Word search (per sound), Sentences, Story game |

The last book of each level from 4 up is a review book. It gets a check-up
per sound and a game, no new teaching. Books at Levels 1 to 3 that already
had hand-made packs get a "More practice" group: check-up, read-draw-write,
game. Sounds a level teaches that no book focuses on (wh; tch dge kn wr mb gn;
ph; sion) sit in a "Level extras" pack.

## What each sheet type is for

- **Trace and write.** Form the sound; trace and write words that use it; write the missing sound.
- **Hear and build.** One box per sound (not per letter); write the missing sound; dictation.
- **Read draw write.** Read a decodable sentence, draw it, copy it. Comprehension without questions.
- **Read in sentences.** Cloze from a word bank, unjumble a sentence, yes/no questions.
- **Spell it.** Circle the correct spelling among plausible alternatives, use it in sentences, dictation.
- **Check-up.** Real or alien (the Year 1 screening skill), speed read three times, dictation. Two wrong means go back a sheet.
- **Fluency.** Roll and read, speed read, real or alien. Repetition, not rushing.
- **Crack the code / Word search.** Decoding under a puzzle, then speed read. For children who won't do another plain sheet.
- **Story game.** Reading race on the book's words. Needs a die and counters. Play twice.

## Quality gates

- Every child-facing word passes the level's decodability guard (hidden digraphs rejected).
- Sheets the forge flags "very few words for this sound" are dropped by `prune_sparse.py`, not shipped.
- Pages are auto-fitted: never clipped, never half-empty.

## Where things live

- PDFs and previews: `public/worksheets/L<level>/<book>/NN_<type>_<sound>.pdf`, plus `pack.pdf`.
- Manifest: `public/worksheets/manifest.json` (the site and the daily drip read this).
- Site data: `src/data/forgedWorksheets.ts`, generated by `write_site_data.py`. The Library's
  worksheet panel merges these into the per-book folders.
- Daily Facebook drip: `scripts/social/build_queue.py` reads the manifest; three sheets a day.

## Adding more later

1. Edit the recipe in `plan.py` (or add a book to the catalogue) and run it.
2. `forge_batch.py` (skips anything already built), then `prune_sparse.py`, `shrink_thumbs.py`.
3. `write_site_data.py`, then `scripts/social/build_queue.py`.
4. Force-add the new PDFs (`*.pdf` is git-ignored) and open a PR.

# MyPhonicsBooks: Meta Ad Framework (Hormozi-Style Direct Response)

## The Formula: Hook > Value > CTA

Every ad follows the same core structure. The hook grabs attention with something familiar (a real headline, a common belief, a recognisable scenario). The value section flips the script and delivers an "oh wow" moment. The CTA drives a specific action.

## 3 Ad Variants

### Ad 1: "The Headline" (News Hook)
Opens with a real, current news headline about the UK reading crisis. Parent sees something they may have scrolled past. Then we flip: "But what if your child doesn't have to be part of this statistic?"

**Hook (0-3s):** Fake screenshot of a news headline: "1 in 5 UK children leave primary school unable to read properly"
**Context (3-7s):** "The government just declared 2026 the National Year of Reading. That's how bad it is."
**Flip (7-11s):** "But the solution isn't more books. It's the RIGHT books. Matched to exactly what they can decode."
**CTA (11-15s):** "Find their exact level in 3 minutes. Free."

### Ad 2: "The Myth" (Belief Breaker)
Opens with a common belief parents hold. Then breaks it with evidence.

**Hook (0-3s):** "Most parents think their child just needs to 'read more'"
**Agitate (3-7s):** "Wrong level books don't build confidence. They kill it."
**Flip (7-11s):** "Every word in a MyPhonicsBooks book is matched to exactly what they can decode. Their name. Their level."
**CTA (11-15s):** "3-minute assessment. 3 free books. Zero risk."

### Ad 3: "The Stat" (Pattern Interrupt)
Opens with a bold, alarming number that fills the screen.

**Hook (0-2s):** Giant "20%" fills screen
**Context (2-5s):** "20% of Year 1 children failed the phonics check this year"
**Question (5-8s):** "Do you know if yours would pass?"
**Solution (8-12s):** "Find out in 3 minutes. Free phonics assessment matched to the UK curriculum."
**CTA (12-15s):** "Get their level + 3 free books"

## Visual Rules (Non-Negotiable)

- NO realistic human faces (photos or detailed illustrations)
- NO music
- Typography-first design
- Book illustrations allowed (Islamic-compliant: minimal/slit eyes only)
- Silhouettes allowed (no facial features)
- Book flat lays and product shots allowed
- Level colours as visual identity markers

## Copy Rules

- British English throughout
- No Oxford commas
- No em dashes
- One message per 3-second block
- Max 5 words per line on screen
- Active verbs, short words, no jargon
- Never say "your child" (use "they" or "them")
- Never use urgency/pressure tactics ("limited time", "act now")

## Colour Palette

- Background: White or soft gradient (light) / Dark slate #0f172a (dark)
- Text: Dark slate #0f172a (on light) / White (on dark)
- Accent: Level colours (Pink #E84B8A, Amber #F59E0B, Green #22C55E, Blue #3B82F6, Purple #8B5CF6, Teal #14B8A6)
- CTA: Gradient indigo-violet
- News headline: White on dark background with red accent bar

## Typography

- Headlines: Plus Jakarta Sans, Bold, 60-80px
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans, Regular, 32-40px
- CTA: Plus Jakarta Sans, Bold, 48px
- Stat numbers: Plus Jakarta Sans, Extra Bold, 120-200px

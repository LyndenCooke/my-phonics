# MyPhonicsBooks — Entry Walkthrough Tutorials

Talking points for the short walkthrough that plays when someone lands or signs up. Two versions: **parents** and **schools**. These are the points to hit, not a script to read word for word. Say them in your own words.

**Length:** 60 to 90 seconds total, roughly one slide every 10 to 15 seconds. Keep it skippable.

**Facts used (current 8-level build, confirm against live site):** 8 levels (L1 to L8), three book types per level (Sound Book, Blending Book, Storybook), 118 books total of which 33 are personalised Storybooks, adaptive assessment, built by a British primary teacher with QTS, mapped to UK, Australian, US Common Core and IB PYP.

---

## PARENT WALKTHROUGH

### Slide 1 — What it is
- Finds your child's exact reading level in minutes
- Then gives them books they can actually read
- No more guessing if a book is too hard or too easy

### Slide 2 — The problem
- Most books are sold by age, not by what your child knows
- An "age 4 to 6" book can be full of sounds they have never been taught
- They meet words they cannot decode, guess, and feel like they are bad at reading
- The fix: match every book to the sounds they already know

### Slide 3 — The assessment
- Quick, friendly, about 3 minutes
- Tests real sounds, real words and tricky words
- Adapts as your child answers
- Places them on a level from 1 to 8
- Shows where they sit against age expectations (most apps hide this)

### Slide 4 — The books
- Every word matched to their level, no surprise words, no guessing
- Three steps per level: Sound Book to learn, Blending Book to practise, Storybook to read
- Stories set all over the world, so your child sees themselves and others
- Not the same suburb and the same character 30 books in a row

### Slide 5 — Honest progress
- Quick Checks confirm they are decoding, not reciting from memory
- They only move up when they have genuinely earned it
- You always see what they know and what is next

### Slide 6 — Get started
- Free assessment now, free personalised book at the end
- No payment to begin
- Tap start and find their level

## SCHOOL WALKTHROUGH

### Slide 1 — What it is
- A decodable reading programme and a teacher platform in one
- Books where every word is decodable, plus a dashboard for every reader
- Built by a practising British primary teacher with QTS, not a software company

### Slide 2 — Whole school on one screen
- Total pupils, who is assessed, who is not, the moment you log in
- See how children spread across the eight levels
- Actions panel flags who needs assessing and who is stuck on a level
- No spreadsheets, no trawling mark books

### Slide 3 — Honest placement
- Adaptive assessment: real sounds, real words, tricky words
- A few minutes per child gives a working level, 1 to 8
- Separates what a child has passed from what was never tested
- The level means something to you and to the parent

### Slide 4 — The teaching pathway
- Same three steps every level: Sound Book, Blending Book, Storybook
- Storybook words are all decodable from sounds taught so far, no surprises
- I-do, we-do, you-do built into the books
- A non-specialist can deliver it well

### Slide 5 — The full programme
- 118 books across 8 levels: 73 Sound, 12 Blending, 33 Storybooks
- Mapped to UK, Australian, US Common Core and IB PYP
- Letters and Sounds underneath, your local curriculum on top
- Matters for international schools reporting against different frameworks

### Slide 6 — Get started
- We load your classes and pupils, so you see your school, not a demo
- Assess every child, see the whole school, give each reader the right book
- Book a short setup to start a trial

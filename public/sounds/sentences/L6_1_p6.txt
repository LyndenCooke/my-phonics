Yusuf stood still for a moment and looked around with new, curious eyes. He could see the enormous pyramids on the far horizon, golden in the dusty afternoon haze. He could hear the joyous calls of the market sellers drifting around the corner. He could smell the fabulous bread from the bakery he had walked past hundreds of times. "It is marvellous," he said, in a quiet, amazed voice. "My neighbourhood is truly marvellous."

They walked on, and the man pointed to the slim minaret that rose above the rooftops. "On quiet mornings, the call from that tower can be heard for miles and miles," he said. Down by the river, white-sailed boats drifted past on the gleaming water. A cluster of street cats — Cairo was famous for its cats — sunned themselves on a warm wall. "Is this place still boring to you?" the man asked, his eyes twinkling.

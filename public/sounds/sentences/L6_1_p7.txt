Uncle Tarek smiled and handed Yusuf a small notebook and a pen.   he said.   Yusuf picked up the pen and began to write, one label for each place he could now see. He wrote: The Marvellous Corniche. The Enormous Nile. The Famous Bakery. The Joyous Market. The Glorious Minaret.   said Yusuf, and he felt proud.

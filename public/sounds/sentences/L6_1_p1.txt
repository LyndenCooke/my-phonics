Yusuf sat on the front steps of his block of flats and frowned at the street below. Rows of cream-coloured blocks lined the road, and cars honked in the hot afternoon heat. From the market around the corner, he could hear the calls of vendors and the clatter of carts. "Nothing glorious or fabulous ever happens here," he muttered. "This place is so ordinary." Just then, a curious-looking man with a worn notebook sat down quietly on the step beside him.

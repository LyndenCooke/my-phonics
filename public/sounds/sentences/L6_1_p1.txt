Yusuf sat on the front steps of his apartment block and frowned at the street below. Rows of cream-coloured blocks lined the road, and cars honked in the hot afternoon heat. From the market around the corner, he could hear the calls of vendors and the clatter of carts. "Nothing glorious or fabulous ever happens here," he muttered. "This place is so ordinary." Just then, his Uncle Tarek sat down quietly on the step beside him, a worn notebook tucked under his arm.

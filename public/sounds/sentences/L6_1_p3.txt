Uncle Tarek stood and walked with Yusuf to the wide road that ran along the edge of the river. "That is the Nile," he said in a wondrous voice, "the most famous river in the whole world." "It has flowed past this very spot for thousands and thousands of years." Yusuf had walked along the corniche countless times, but he had never stopped to wonder. He looked at the grey-green water as if he was seeing it for the very first time.

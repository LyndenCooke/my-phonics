Next, Nonna filled a big pot with hot water and set it on the stove. She tipped in long strands of fresh pasta and stirred them with a wooden spoon. "Pasta al limone," she announced. "My mother's precious secret — lemon, cream, and a sprinkle of pepper." The steam that rose from the pot smelled absolutely delicious, and Luca found himself stepping closer, even though he was still cautious. "I am not eating that," he said, but his feet did not step back.

"Mia!" Tom whispered. "Come here — but be flexible and go slowly!" Mia came back along the trail, looking suspicious. But when she crouched beside Tom and saw the lyrebird, her face changed. "That is remarkable," she breathed. "I had no idea it was there." She was conscious now that she had rushed past something precious. Being fast did not make her more capable — it just meant she had missed the most valuable thing on the whole walk.

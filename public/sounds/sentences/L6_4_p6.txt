After the lyrebird slipped back into the bush, they walked on together. This time, Mia let Tom set the pace. He showed her observable things she had never noticed — a notable pattern in the bark of a gum tree, a tiny scrumptious bush berry that was edible, and the delicious smell of eucalyptus oil hanging in the warm air.   Mia said, and she meant it. The glorious view from the lookout was even better when they reached it side by side.

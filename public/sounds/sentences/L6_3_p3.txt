Nonna drizzled green oil over the sliced tomatoes and torn basil leaves. Then she grated fresh lemon rind on top and added a pinch of salt. "This is bruschetta," she said. "It is simple and nutritious." Luca peered at the crusty bread stacked on a plate beside the dish. He picked one up and inspected it. "It looks suspicious," he said again. But the smell was delicious — fresh and bright and sharp.

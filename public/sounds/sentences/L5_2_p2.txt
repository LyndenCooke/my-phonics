I went in under the trees. The forest floor was soft with fresh snow. I could hear the wind up in the branches. Then I heard something else — a small crunch, near a big pine tree. I stopped and kept still. I did not move a bit. What was it? I crept near. And there, sticking out from behind the tree, I could see one thick bushy tail. Just one fluffy tail. Red and soft, with a snowy tip.

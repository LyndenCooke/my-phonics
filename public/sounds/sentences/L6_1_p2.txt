"You look like somebody who thinks they have seen everything there is to see," said the man with a smile. Yusuf shrugged. "I live here. It is just houses, traffic and the same old market." The man had a luminous look in his eyes, as if he knew something tremendous. "I have spent my whole life travelling to marvellous places," he said, leaning forward. "And I am going to tell you something. This is one of the most glorious cities on Earth."

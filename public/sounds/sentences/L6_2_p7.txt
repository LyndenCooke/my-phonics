The guard came running back with a woman and a man following close behind. The little boy looked up — and in less than a second, his face went from frightened to overjoyed. "Mama!" he cried, and the woman rushed forward and scooped him up, clutching him in an incredible, tearful hug. The man gripped the guard's hand and then looked at the girl with a grateful and disbelieving expression. She stood a little way back, watching the most remarkable reunion she had ever seen, with something warm and steady building inside her.

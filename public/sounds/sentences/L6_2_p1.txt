The Lantern Festival had filled the river park with golden light. Hundreds of glowing lanterns drifted above the Li River, and the karst mountains rose like dark peaks behind the city. The girl was watching a paper lantern float upwards when she noticed something terrible. A small boy sat alone on a stone step, clutching a stuffed panda, with tears on his face. The people all around him walked past as if he were invisible. She stopped.

Tom crept off the trail, cautious and sensible, placing each foot with care. Behind a thick fern, he saw something wondrous — a lyrebird. Its enormous tail feathers fanned out like a gorgeous silver veil. The bird lifted its head and sang the rosella's call, then the call of a bird Tom could not see, then a sound like a creaking branch. It was performing its famous song, copying every creature in the bush. It was the most incredible thing Tom had ever seen. He held his breath and sketched it in his notebook as fast as he could.

She pulled a small notebook from her jacket pocket and thought for a moment. Then she wrote a careful note in neat, readable handwriting and tore it out. The note said: "Lost child — dark jacket, green buttons, age 3. He is by the red lantern at the bridge. His family is searching for him. If you have seen them, please come at once." She held it up as high as she could. It was a sensible and responsible plan.

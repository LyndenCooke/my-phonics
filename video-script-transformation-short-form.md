# MyPhonicsBooks — Short-Form Video Script

**Platform:** Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts
**Length:** 60–90 seconds
**Audience:** Parents of children aged 4–8
**Goal:** Build authority
**Angle:** Transformation story
**Framework:** Kallaway's 4-Step Addiction Loop (Stakes → Big Question → Head Fake → Rehook)

---

## SCRIPT

### HOOK + STAKES (0–10s)

**[Visual: Child looking frustrated with a reading book, turning away]**

> "Most parents think their child doesn't like reading."

**[Beat. Direct to camera or voiceover.]**

> "But I've taught hundreds of children to read, and I've seen the same thing happen over and over again."

---

### BIG QUESTION (10–25s)

**[Visual: Cut to a child picking up a different book — brighter, personalised cover]**

> "Watch what happens when you give a child a book where every single word is one they can actually sound out."

**[Visual: Child starts reading aloud. Fluent. Confident. Maybe a small smile.]**

> "No guessing. No skipping words. No asking mum for help every other line."

**[Visual: Child turns the page independently.]**

> "They just... read."

---

### HEAD FAKE (25–50s)

**[Visual: Cut back to presenter / voiceover with text overlay]**

> "Here's what most people get wrong. They think the problem is that their child needs more practice."

**[Beat.]**

> "It's not a practice problem. It's a matching problem."

**[Visual: Side-by-side — a generic reading book vs. a MyPhonicsBooks PDF with the child's name on it]**

> "Most books throw in words children haven't been taught yet. So the child guesses, gets it wrong, and starts to believe they're bad at reading."

> "But when every word in the book is decodable at their exact phonics level — something clicks. They stop guessing and start reading."

---

### REHOOK + CTA (50–70s)

**[Visual: Stack of personalised books fanning out / child reading proudly]**

> "That moment — when a child realises they can do it on their own — that's not just reading. That's confidence."

**[Beat. Lean in.]**

> "The question is: do you know exactly which phonics level your child is at right now?"

**[Visual: Phone screen showing assessment / book generation]**

> "We built a free 3-minute assessment that tells you. Then it generates 5 personalised books matched to your child's level. Every word decodable. Their name in the story."

**[Text overlay: "Link in bio" or "Free assessment — link below"]**

> "Link's in the bio."

## FRAMEWORK BREAKDOWN

| Loop Step | How it's used in this script |
|-----------|------------------------------|
| **Stakes** | "Most parents think their child doesn't like reading" — implies something is being misunderstood, child's confidence is at risk |
| **Big Question** | "Watch what happens when..." — viewer predicts what the transformation looks like |
| **Head Fake** | It's not a practice problem, it's a matching problem — breaks the expected parental assumption |
| **Rehook** | "Do you know which phonics level your child is at right now?" — opens a new personal loop the viewer needs to resolve |

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Tone: warm, knowledgeable, slightly conspiratorial (like sharing something most people miss)
- Pacing: conversational but tight. No filler words.
- B-roll ideas: child reading fluently, close-up of book pages with decodable words highlighted, the personalised cover, the assessment on a phone screen
- Music: gentle, uplifting underscore — builds subtly during the transformation moment
- Text overlays: key phrases ("It's not a practice problem", "It's a matching problem", "Every word decodable")
- Caption style: punchy subtitles throughout (essential for sound-off viewing)

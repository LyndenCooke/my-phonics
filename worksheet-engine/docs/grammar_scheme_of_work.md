# MyPhonicsBooks: Grammar Scheme of Work (L1 to L8)

The grammar strand is its own **separate workbook** at every level, the way a published grammar scheme runs alongside phonics rather than inside it. This document is the scope and sequence: what each Grammar booklet teaches, in what order, and how it maps to the National Curriculum. Phonics, handwriting and spelling are separate booklets with their own schemes.

**Status:** scope and sequence for review. No worksheets built yet.
**Aligned to:** National Curriculum (England) English programmes of study KS1, English Appendix 2 (Vocabulary, Grammar and Punctuation), and Year 3 grammar for L8 readiness. British English throughout.
**Pedagogy:** every unit follows **I do, We do, You do** (model, guided, independent) and Rosenshine's small steps. Statutory terminology is taught explicitly and used with the children.

## 1. How the grammar strand maps to the curriculum

Our 8 reading levels span Reception to Year 3 readiness. Statutory grammar begins in Year 1, so the early levels lay the **pre-statutory foundations** (EYFS Literacy and Communication) that Year 1 grammar assumes.

| Level | Colour | Curriculum band | Grammar status |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 Ditties | Pink | Reception (early) | Pre-statutory foundations: concept of word and sentence, oral composition |
| L2 First Sounds | Coral | Reception | Pre-statutory: capital letters, full stops, the pronoun I emerging |
| L3 Special Friends | Amber | Reception (late) | Pre-statutory into Y1: proper nouns, question marks emerging |
| L4 Longer Sounds | Green | Year 1 | Year 1 grammar (statutory) begins properly |
| L5 New Spellings | Blue | Year 1 into early Y2 | Year 1 grammar secured, first Year 2 concepts |
| L6 Building Fluency | Indigo | Year 2 | Year 2 grammar (statutory) |
| L7 Reading Together | Purple | Year 2 | Year 2 grammar mastery |
| L8 Reading Champion | Teal | Year 2 mastery, Year 3 readiness | Year 2 to mastery, Year 3 grammar introduced |

## 2. The five grammar domains (the spiral strands)

Every Grammar booklet draws from the same five domains so progression is visible and skills spiral. A child meets each domain again at every level, at a higher level of demand.

1. **Word structure and word classes**: plurals, suffixes, prefixes, noun, verb, adjective, adverb
2. **Sentence structure**: what a sentence is, co-ordination, subordination, sentence types, expanded noun phrases, sentence variety
3. **Text and cohesion**: oral composition, sequencing, time adverbials, tense consistency, paragraphs
4. **Punctuation**: finger spaces, capital letters, full stops, question and exclamation marks, commas, apostrophes, inverted commas
5. **Statutory terminology**: the grammar vocabulary the NC requires children to know and use at each year

## 3. The Grammar booklet structure (every level, same shape)

Each level's Grammar workbook is standalone, like a Schofield and Sims or CGP grammar book:

1. **Cover**: level colour, "Grammar" strand, level mascot
2. **Contents**: the units in this booklet
3. **How to use**: the I do / We do / You do routine, for the parent or teacher
4. **Units**: the grammar units below, each one a worksheet (1 to 2 pages). Each worksheet carries: a clear objective, a modelled example (I do), a guided task (We do), an independent task (You do)
5. **Review / Challenge**: a mixed SPaG page in NC test format
6. **Answers**: a teacher and parent answer key (back of booklet)
7. **Certificate**: grammar award for the level

---

## 4. Worksheet design rules for the grammar strand

- **Decodable rule still applies.** Example words and sentences in the booklet must be decodable at that reading level or use that level's tricky words. This is the constraint that makes our grammar scheme different from a generic one: a child can read every example independently. It binds tightest at L1 to L5 and relaxes as the code completes.
- **Sentence-length bands** (from the Curriculum Ledger): L1 3-5 words, L2 3-6, L3 4-7, L4 4-8, L5 5-10, L6 6-12, L7 8-15, L8 varied.
- **Statutory terminology** is used with the children, not hidden. Each unit names the term it teaches.
- **Activity formats** come from the grammar activity bank: tick one of four, draw-a-line match, insert the missing mark, circle or highlight the target, cut and sort into columns, cloze with a word bank, rewrite and improve, hold a sentence, challenge mat. Each unit below names the format(s) it uses.
- British English spelling and punctuation. No grammar structure exceeds the writing track for the level.

| Question and exclamation marks | L3-L4 | L4-L6 | L7 |

## 7. Exit criteria per Grammar booklet

Each criterion states the skill **and what counts as secure** (the measure the Review page checks), so a booklet works as an intervention, not just a worksheet folder. "Secure" is the bar for moving to the next level; below it, revisit.

- **L1 secure:** in 3 of 3 caption tasks, says the sentence aloud, then copies it leaving a finger space between every word.
- **L2 secure:** in 4 of 5 sentences, starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop; capitalises the pronoun I in 3 of 3 cases.
- **L3 secure:** capitalises the name in 4 of 5 sentences; chooses the correct end mark (. or ?) in 4 of 5 sentences.
- **L4 secure:** joins two ideas with "and" in 4 of 5 prompts; chooses the correct end mark (. ? !) in 4 of 5; forms the plural (-s or -es) in 4 of 5.
- **L5 secure:** applies the -ing / -ed doubling rule in 4 of 5; uses the prefix un- correctly in 4 of 5; places commas correctly in 2 of 2 lists; spots the words before a noun in 4 of 5.
- **L6 secure:** identifies the sentence type and adds the correct end punctuation in 4 of 5 sentences; builds an expanded noun phrase with at least one adjective in 4 of 5; keeps tense consistent across a 4-sentence text.
- **L7 secure:** uses the singular possessive apostrophe in 4 of 5; chooses the right homophone in 4 of 5; writes a recount of 4 or more sentences using at least 3 time adverbials.
- **L8 secure (Year 2 mastery):** sorts singular and plural possessives in 4 of 5; finds and fixes at least 4 of 5 errors in a proofreading paragraph; varies a sentence opener in 2 of 3 ways.
- **L8 working towards (Year 3 readiness, not required to pass L8):** uses a fronted adverbial with a comma in 2 of 2 attempts; adds inverted commas correctly to 2 lines of dialogue.

## 8. Build order for the grammar strand

1. Approve this scope and sequence
2. Build the **L6 Grammar booklet** first as the worked exemplar: it is the richest Year 2 statutory band and exercises most worksheet formats (cut and sort, cloze, build, rewrite, circle). Proving it proves the strand.
3. Build the shared grammar templates (tick one of four, draw-a-line match, insert the missing, cut and sort, cloze, rewrite and improve, challenge mat) to the locked worksheet spec
4. Roll out L4, L5, L7 (statutory Y1 and Y2), then L1-L3 foundations, then L8 readiness
5. Cross-check every example against the decodable rule and the sentence-length band for its level

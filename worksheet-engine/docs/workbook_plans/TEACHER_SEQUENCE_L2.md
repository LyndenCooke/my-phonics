# Teaching Sequence — Level 2 First Sounds (website copy) — v3

For the website. Level 2 finishes the first sounds: nineteen graphemes over
five books in six weeks, one book a week with week 6 for the assessment.
About FIVE MINUTES of workbook a day.

## What is in the six weeks

- Five reading books: The Red Socks (c k ck e) · Run, Pup, Run! (u r h b) ·
  Fox Fell Off! (f ff l ll) · The Jam Jug (ss j v w) · The Yak and the Box
  (x y z, the round-up).
- Nineteen sound books, one per grapheme, each before or with its workbook
  sound page.
- One workbook: each book WEAVES one sound page, then one writing page —
  for example The sound c · grammar · The sound k · Sentences · The sound
  ck · Spell it · The sound e · Big write · Handwriting.
- Grammar lives one unit per book: capital letters · full stops · the word
  I · is it a sentence? The round-up book carries Fix and answer instead.
- Big writes vary: books 2 and 4 use the sequencing page (number the four
  story pictures in order, then write); the others use one big scene.

## The weeks, book by book

| Week | Book | Sounds | Grammar page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Red Socks | c k ck e | Capital letters start sentences |
| 2 | Run, Pup, Run! | u r h b | Full stops end sentences |
| 3 | Fox Fell Off! | f ff l ll | The word I |
| 4 | The Jam Jug | ss j v w | Is it a sentence? |
| 5 | The Yak and the Box | x y z | Fix and answer (round-up) |
| 6 | Assessment | — | Show what you know A + B |

Within a week: sound book then sound page on alternating days; the writing
pages fill the days between; Spell it practise the day before its test;
the Big write closes the book.

## Spelling test words

Six per book from its focus sounds plus the Level 2 tricky words (no, go,
to, into, is); the half-term list draws across all five books. The words
are printed on the Spelling words page at the back.

# Teaching Sequence — Level 5 New Spellings (website copy) — v3

For the website. Level 5 brings the split digraphs and the first
alternative spellings: five books in six weeks, one book a week with week 6
for the assessment. About FIVE MINUTES of workbook a day. Handwriting
moves to its own slot from here — pre-cursive flicks begin.

## What is in the six weeks

- Five reading books: The Big Bike Race (a-e i-e) · Lost at the Night
  Market (o-e u-e) · The Dream Team (ea ie) · What Min Saw (oi aw) · The
  Boat with the Red Sail (ai oa).
- Ten sound books, one per spelling, before or with their reading book.
- One workbook: per book — grammar · Sentences · Answer it in a sentence ·
  Use your grammar · Spell it · Big write · Handwriting.
- Grammar: double the letter, add the ending · the prefix un- · commas in
  lists · put it in order · nouns and verbs · build the noun phrase.
- Big writes vary: books 2 and 4 use the sequencing page (number the four
  story pictures in order, then write with First, Next, Then and Last);
  the others use one big scene.

## The weeks, book by book

| Week | Book | Grammar pages |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Big Bike Race | Double the letter, then add the ending |
| 2 | Lost at the Night Market | The prefix un- |
| 3 | The Dream Team | Commas in lists |
| 4 | What Min Saw | Put it in order |
| 5 | The Boat with the Red Sail | Nouns and verbs · Build the noun phrase |
| 6 | Assessment | Show what you know A + B |

Within a week: read the book across two or three days; one workbook page a
day in printed order; Spell it practise the day before its test; the Big
write closes the book.

## Spelling test words

Ten per book from its focus spellings plus the Level 5 tricky words; the
half-term list draws across all five books. The words are printed on the
Spelling words page at the back.

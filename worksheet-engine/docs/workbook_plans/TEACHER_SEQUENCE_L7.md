# Teaching Sequence — Level 7 Reading Together (website copy) — v3

For the website. Level 7 is greater-depth territory: the late trigraphs
(ire ore ear oor ure tion) and richer sentence work. Four books over the
six weeks. About FIVE MINUTES of workbook a day; joined handwriting is
settling in.

## What is in the six weeks

- Four reading books: Before the Shore (ore ire) · Near the Door (ear oor)
  · Sure She Can! (ure tion) · A Place for Me (review).
- Six sound books, one per trigraph, before or with their reading book.
- One workbook: per book — grammar · Sentences · Answer it in a sentence ·
  Use your grammar · Spell it · Big write · Handwriting.
- Grammar: the possessive apostrophe · homophones · tell it in order ·
  was and -ing · sort the suffix · be a proofreader.
- Big writes vary: books 2 and 4 use the sequencing page (number the four
  story pictures in order, then write with First, Next, Then and Last);
  the others use one big scene.

## The weeks, book by book

| Week | Book | Grammar pages |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before the Shore | The possessive apostrophe |
| 2 | Near the Door | Homophones |
| 3-4 | Sure She Can! | Tell it in order · Was and -ing |
| 5 | A Place for Me | Sort the suffix · Be a proofreader |
| 6 | Assessment | Show what you know A + B |

Within a week: read the book across two or three days; one workbook page a
day in printed order; Spell it practise the day before its test; the Big
write closes the book.

## Spelling test words

Ten per book from its focus spellings plus the Level 7 tricky words; the
half-term list draws across all four books. The words are printed on the
Spelling words page at the back.

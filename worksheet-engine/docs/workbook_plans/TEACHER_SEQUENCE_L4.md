# Teaching Sequence — Level 4 Longer Sounds (website copy) — v3

For the website. Level 4 brings the vowel digraphs: eleven longer sounds
over six books in six weeks, one book a week. About FIVE MINUTES of
workbook a day. From this level the sound work lives in the sound books;
the workbook concentrates on writing.

## What is in the six weeks

- Six reading books: The Night Light (ay igh) · Hot Food, Cool Moon (oo ow) ·
  Morning on the Farm (ar or) · The Fair in the Air (air ir) · Round and
  Round (ou oy) · The Night Fair (review).
- Eleven sound books, one per grapheme, before or with their reading book.
- One workbook: per book — grammar · Sentences · Answer it in a sentence ·
  Use your grammar · Spell it · Big write · Handwriting. The review book
  carries Fix and answer instead of new grammar.
- Grammar: joining with and · choose the end mark · one and more than one ·
  capital letters for days · add -ing, -ed, -er.
- Big writes vary: books 2, 4 and 6 use the sequencing page (number the
  four story pictures in order, then write what happens, in order); the
  others use one big scene.

## The weeks, book by book

| Week | Book | Grammar page |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Night Light | Joining with and |
| 2 | Hot Food, Cool Moon | Choose the end mark |
| 3 | Morning on the Farm | One and more than one |
| 4 | The Fair in the Air | Capital letters for days |
| 5 | Round and Round | Add -ing, -ed, -er |
| 6 | The Night Fair + assessment | Fix and answer · Show what you know A + B |

Within a week: read the book across two or three days; one workbook page a
day in printed order; Spell it practise the day before its test; the Big
write closes the book.

## Spelling test words

Eight per book from its focus sounds plus the Level 4 tricky words; the
half-term list draws across all six books. The words are printed on the
Spelling words page at the back.

# MyPhonicsBooks — Booklet running order (per level)

Companion to `booklet_plan.md`. That doc lists pages grouped by strand. **This doc is the teaching order**: the exact sheet-by-sheet sequence a child works through, level by level.

## How the order works

A level is made of **books** (L1 has 2, L2 has 5, and so on). The booklet runs **book by book**. Each book is a small repeating teaching cycle, the **Book Pack**, always in the same order so the routine is familiar:

1. **Sound sheet** — one Single-Sound sheet per focus grapheme of that book (the locked master)
2. **Blend it** — build words from those sounds (becomes a spelling-pattern sort from L4)
3. **Alien words** — nonsense words using those sounds, for Phonics Screening Check practice (L1 to L6 only; retired after)
4. **Handwriting** — letter formation early on, joins from L5
5. **Tricky words** — the new tricky or common-exception words for that level (introduced across the early books, revisited later)
6. **Grammar** — one mini-skill from that level's grammar track
7. **Sentence writing** — "Say it. Tap it. Write it. Check it." with finger spaces emphasised at L1 to L4
8. **Book-linked sheet** — comprehension or retrieval tied to that actual storybook (from L2 upward)

Not every book runs all eight every time. The **component lifecycle** in the Curriculum Ledger decides which are active at each level (for example: no alien words past L6, handwriting joins only from L5, comprehension grows from a light touch at L2 to core from L5).

**Two ways to print:** each Book Pack can print as a standalone mini-pack, or all the book packs bind together with the front and back matter into one level booklet. That keeps a Reception booklet from becoming unwieldy.

> Page counts here are higher than in `booklet_plan.md` because every book now carries its own full cycle (blend, alien, handwriting, grammar, sentence, book-linked), not one shared block per level. If you want a lighter pack, the quickest trims are: merge **Blend + Alien** onto one page, and run **one grammar page per two books** instead of every book. Flagged for your call.

Legend: `SOUND` single-sound sheet · `BLEND` word building · `ALIEN` nonsense words · `HAND` handwriting · `TRICKY` tricky/CEW words · `SPELL` spelling sort/suffix · `GRAMMAR` grammar mini-skill · `SENTENCE` sentence writing · `BOOK` book-linked activity · `REVIEW`/`CHALLENGE`/`CERT` back matter.

## Level 1 — Ditties (Pink) · 2 books · sounds s a t p i n m d g o

**Front matter**
1. COVER — pink, L1.1 mascot
2. CONTENTS
3. HOW-TO — "Say it. Tap it. Write it. Check it."
4. SOUND MAT — all 10 Phase 2 sounds

**Book L1.1 — "Tap! Tap! Tap!" (s a t p i n)**
5. SOUND — s
6. SOUND — a
7. SOUND — t
8. SOUND — p
9. SOUND — i
10. SOUND — n
11. BLEND — build CVC words (sat, tap, pin, nip, tin)
12. ALIEN — nonsense words (sip, nat, pid, tig)
13. HAND — letter families for these letters (start-dot + arrow)
14. TRICKY — introduce I, the
15. GRAMMAR — caption: picture + "Say it, then write it"
16. SENTENCE — copy a caption with finger spaces ("I sit.")
17. BOOK — match picture to caption from the book

**Book L1.2 — "The Mud on the Dog" (m d g o)**
18. SOUND — m
19. SOUND — d
20. SOUND — g
21. SOUND — o
22. BLEND — CVC words (mud, dog, mop, got, dim)
23. ALIEN — nonsense words (mog, dap, gom, nod)
24. HAND — formation for m d g o
25. SENTENCE — copy a caption with finger spaces ("The dog sat.")
26. BOOK — find today's sound in the book

**Back matter**
27. REVIEW — circle the sound · trace the letter · copy the caption
28. CERT — "L1 Ditties completed"

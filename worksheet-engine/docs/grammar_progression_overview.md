## How the strand climbs to L6 (and past it)

The grammar strand is a spiral: each level meets the same five domains again at a
higher demand, and every example stays decodable for that reading level. The early
levels lay the **pre-statutory foundations** that Year-1/2 grammar assumes — so by L6
(Year 2 statutory) the child already owns sentence/word concepts, capitals, end
marks, names, "and"-joining, plurals and simple suffixes, and is ready for sentence
types, expanded noun phrases, full co-ordination/subordination, apostrophes and tense.

| Level | Band | Booklet focus | What it adds on the way up |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** Foundations | Reception | word & sentence concept, finger spaces | *what a word/sentence even is* — the ground everything stands on |
| **L2** First Punctuation | Reception | capital to start, full stop to end, capital **I** | the sentence gets a shape: starts big, ends with a stop |
| **L3** Names & Questions | Reception→Y1 | capitals for names, **?**, statement vs question | sentences now have *kinds*; proper nouns appear |
| **L4** Joining & Endings | **Year 1** | join with **and**, choose `. ? !`, day capitals, plurals, **-ing/-ed/-er** | first statutory grammar: clauses join, words take endings |
| **L5** Word Building & Order | Y1→early Y2 | doubling suffixes, prefix **un-**, commas in lists, sequencing, noun/verb, words-before-a-noun | word morphology + the *simple noun phrase* that L6 will expand |
| **L6** Sentence Types & Phrases | **Year 2** ★ | 4 sentence types, **expanded** noun phrases, and/but/or/so, when/if/that/because, adjective/adverb, contractions, tense | the full Year-2 statutory band — the exemplar everything aims at |
| **L7** Apostrophes & Cohesion | Y2 mastery | possessive **'s**, homophones, time adverbials, progressive tense, suffix families | secures Y2 and adds cohesion/possession |
| **L8** Mastery & Y3 readiness | Y2→Y3 | singular vs plural possession, opener variety, proofreading + **[Y3 next step]** fronted adverbials, speech marks, time/place/cause | mastery + a clearly-flagged stretch into Year 3 |

Each booklet opens its Review with a quick retrieval of the **previous level's**
secured skills before the new units, so the climb is explicit (see scheme §6 spiral
schedule and §7 exit criteria).

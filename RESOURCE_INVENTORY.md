## Resource Type 1: Sound Books (73 total)

**Format:** A5 booklet. Cover page, sound page ("Today we are learning..."), 4–6 word pages with real photographs, "Read Them All" checklist, back cover.

**Purpose:** Introduce ONE new grapheme-phoneme correspondence (GPC). Child sees the grapheme, hears the sound, then sees real photos of words containing that sound.

**At L5+:** Sound Books also show comparison sounds (alternative spellings of the same phoneme) so the child sees the full "sound family".

**Status:** All 73 data files complete. PDF generation pipeline exists. Some PDFs built, others pending.

## Resource Type 2: Blending Books (12 total)

**Format:** Small A6 illustrated booklet. Child sees the word on one page, blends it ("sound it out"), turns the page and sees the word with an illustration to check. 5–8 words per book.

**Purpose:** Bridge between Sound Books and Storybooks. Practises blending the newly taught GPCs in isolation before the child meets them inside a story. Ensures the storybook becomes a fluency read, not a decoding struggle.

**Note:** No Blending Books at L6–L8. By that stage, children blend in context inside the storybooks.

**Status:** All 12 data files complete. PDF template exists. PDFs not yet generated.

1. Blending Book 1 — s a t p (L1, CVC with s, a, t, p)
2. Blending Book 2 — SATPIN + MDGO (L1, CVC with all 10 L1 sounds)
3. Blending Book 3 — c k ck e u r h b (L2, CVC with new consonants)

## Teaching Pathway — How Resources Connect

The teaching sequence at each level follows this pattern:

```
Sound Books (introduce GPCs one at a time)
    ↓
Blending Book (practise blending those GPCs in isolated words)
    ↓
Storybook (apply those GPCs in a real narrative)
    ↓
Worksheets (reinforce through writing, sorting, comprehension)
```

Resources are grouped into blocks within each level. A block typically has 3–6 Sound Books, then a Blending Book, then 1–2 Storybooks. The final storybook at L4, L7 and L8 is a review book that consolidates all sounds from that level.

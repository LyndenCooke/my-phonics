## How companion resources attach to the pathway

### At the start of each level

When a child begins a new level, the teacher should have:

- **Sound mat** for that level — a cumulative chart showing all GPCs taught at this level and all earlier levels. Pinned to the table during reading sessions.
- **Tricky word cards** for that level — cut-and-stick flashcards for the new tricky words introduced at this level.

These are not teaching steps. They are reference materials that stay on the table throughout the level.

### Alongside each Sound Book

Every Sound Book has a matching worksheet (1–2 pages):
- Sound formation (how to write the grapheme)
- Word build (make words with the new sound)
- Sound sort (sort words by sound)

So when the pathway says "Sound Book: sh", the teacher actually uses:
1. Sound Book: sh (real-photo book introducing the sound)
2. Sound Book worksheet: sh (1–2 page reinforcement sheet)

### Alongside each Storybook

Every storybook has two companion resources:

**Storybook worksheet pack (5 A4 pages):**
- Sound hunt (find words with the focus sound in the story)
- Trace and write (handwriting practice with story words)
- Read and do (comprehension activity)
- Alien words (nonsense word blending for Phonics Screening Check preparation)
- Sound sort or story-and-draw

**Interactive digital version (web-based reader) containing:**
- Tappable words with phoneme pop-ups and audio playback
- Vocabulary preview before reading
- Sound spotlight page (focus grapheme with example words and images; at L5+ shows dual-variant columns for graphemes with multiple sounds)
- Story pages with audio
- Spelling drag-and-drop activity
- Story ordering activity (drag images into correct sequence)
- Multiple-choice comprehension quiz
- Grammar word-order activity (drag words to build sentences, from L2+)
- Writing practice
- Nonsense words challenge
- Reading Star certificate

Plus the storybook itself already contains built-in practice pages:
- Combined activity page (comprehension questions, "Can You Read?" word list, "Draw Your Favourite Part")
- Writing practice page (4-line handwriting)
- Nonsense words challenge page
- Reading Star certificate page

### Alongside each Blending Book

Blending Books do not have separate worksheets. The blending practice IS the resource.

## Worksheet component lifecycle

Not all worksheet types are used at every level. The programme phases components in and out:

| Worksheet Component | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 | L5 | L6 | L7 | L8 |
|---------------------|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Letter tracing | Core | Core | Core | Recap | — | — | — | — |
| Handwriting (joining) | — | — | Intro | Intro | Core | Core | Core | Recap |
| Alien / nonsense words | Core | Core | Core | Core | Core | Light | — | — |
| Tricky word practise | Core | Core | Core | Core | Core | Core | Core | Light |
| Sentence and grammar | Light | Light | Intro | Intro | Core | Core | Core | Core |
| Comprehension | — | Light | Light | Intro | Core | Core | Core | Core |
| Vocabulary and spelling | — | — | — | Light | Intro | Core | Core | Core |

This means early levels focus on letter formation and blending. Later levels shift towards comprehension, grammar and vocabulary. The worksheet packs for each storybook draw from whichever components are active at that level.

## What this means for the pathway visual

When you show the pathway on the website or in a proposal:

1. **Do not just count books.** Count all resources: 313+, not 118.
2. **Show companion resources alongside each step**, not as separate sections.
3. **Show the sound mat and tricky word cards at the start of each level**, not hidden in a separate "resources" page.
4. **Show the interactive book as the digital companion to the printed storybook**, not as a separate product.
5. **Show the worksheet lifecycle** so schools can see that the programme matures with the child: letter tracing phases out, comprehension phases in.

The correct marketing line is:

**313+ resources across 8 levels: 73 Sound Books with real photographs, 12 Blending Books, 33 illustrated Storybooks with interactive digital versions, 106 matched worksheets, 8 sound mats and 8 tricky word card sets. Every resource is linked to a precise step in a 118-step teaching pathway.**

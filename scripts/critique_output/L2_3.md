### 2. Narrative critique
- The story arc is clear: trip to a farm, some work, night exploration, a surprise new kid, happy close. Text mostly matches images.
- Several sentences feel forced or padded for sound coverage:
  - “I see a yard with corn in a jar.” is long and slightly clunky.
  - “I get a fork for the garden.” plus “Good food for the farm.” reads like set‑up lines rather than natural speech.
  - Repetition “I dig, dig, dig!” works for energy but may be padding.
- Image–text mismatches/risks:
  - Page 5 uses “door” (not allowed) and focuses on the door; image shows walking with a torch, so “go to the barn” works better.
  - Page 6 “corner” (not allowed) tries to describe the shadow’s location; image can be covered without that word.

### 3. Level appropriateness
- Original does not meet spec of two sentences per page (pages 3, 4 and 6 have three).
- Grapheme coverage is good and foregrounds ar/or, with plenty of cumulative sounds (oo, ee, ow, ir).
- A small number of harder two‑syllable items (“garden”, “morning”) are present; “morning” is fine; “garden” is borderline but decodable.

### 1. Phonics audit
Page 1
- I sit on a big log. Buzz, buzz, buzz!
- All words fully decodable or on the tricky-word list (I, a). “buzz” uses zz (allowed).

Page 2
- A big bug sits on a rock. Hiss, hiss, hiss!
- All words decodable. Verb -s in “sits” is permitted. “rock” uses ck (allowed). “hiss” uses ss (allowed).

Page 3
- I sing a long, long song!
- All words decodable. sing/long/song use ng (allowed).

Page 4
- I sing! No buzz! No hiss!
- All words decodable/tricky. “no” is on the tricky list.

Page 5
- I sing quick! I sing and sing!
- All words decodable. “quick” uses qu and ck (allowed). “and” is permitted (final nd blend allowed).

Page 6
- Buzz! Hiss! I sing with the bugs!
- All words decodable/tricky. “with” uses th (allowed). Plural -s in “bugs” is permitted.

Conclusion: No phonics violations.

### 2. Narrative critique
- Text alone mostly tells a simple story, but there are issues:
  - Page 4 image shows the child quiet with a “shh” gesture, but the first clause says “I sing!” which contradicts the image and the following “No buzz! No hiss!”
  - The cause-and-effect of the sounds stopping and then returning is unclear.
- Repetition works for rhythm and decoding practice, but several pages have two sentences and some sentences exceed the 3–5 word target, so pacing feels uneven.
- Onomatopoeia fits the images. However, “hiss” for the green bug may be less intuitive than “chirp”, though “chirp” is not yet decodable.

### 3. Level appropriateness
- Current book breaks programme specs:
  - Sentences per page: several pages have two sentences (target is one).
  - Words per sentence: some have 6–7 words.
- Phonics coverage is strong: ng, ss, zz, qu are present and earlier graphemes are reused.
- Words/book: With 6 pages and a 1‑sentence, 3–5 word limit, the maximum achievable is ~30 words, so the stated 40–80 target cannot be met unless page count increases. As-is, the manuscript sits below that target.

### 4. Proposed rewrite
Goal: one natural-sounding sentence per page (3–5 words), matching the images and foregrounding ss/zz/qu/ng.

Page 1
- I sit on a log.

Page 2
- A bug can buzz.

Page 3
- I sing a song.

Page 4
- No buzz, no hiss.

Page 5
- I sing quick, quick!

Page 6
- I sing with the bugs.

Total: 26 words; all decodable/tricky.

### 5. Notes for the human editor
- “Bee/bees” isn’t available (ee not yet taught), so I used “bug/bugs” to refer to the flying insects in the art.
- If you want stronger qu coverage, Page 2 could be “A bug is quick.” and Page 5 changed to “I sing and sing.” Both versions remain fully decodable and natural.
- The silence image on Page 4 is now aligned with “No buzz, no hiss.” If you keep the original art pose (finger on lips), this line fits well.
- The 40–80 word target cannot be reached with 6 pages at 3–5 words each; consider either allowing two short sentences on some pages or increasing page count in future titles.
